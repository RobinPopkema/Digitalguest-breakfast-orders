Breakfast Orders 2.0.29 — updater verification

This is a version-only test update for verifying the updater in 2.0.28. Application behavior, print layouts and saved-data format are unchanged.

In version 2.0.28, open Settings > About & licenses > Check for updates. Close Settings, then click Update available and confirm Update and restart. After restarting, About & licenses should show 2.0.29. Your orders and settings should remain intact.

Versions 2.0.26 and 2.0.27 still require a manual ZIP replacement because their updater launcher is faulty.
